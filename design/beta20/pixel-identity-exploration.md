# Syphon pixel identity exploration

Three concept directions generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the current hybrid-v3 orbital-S emblem as the visual reference. Existing approved branding is preserved and no application assets are replaced.

1. Orbital S: closest continuity with the existing ribbon around a black globe. Recommended starting point for a pixel adaptation.
2. Pixel Siphon: downward transfer arrow and receiving tray with an S-shaped negative-space detail.
3. Circuit S: interlocking stepped S monogram, a stronger departure from the existing silhouette.

The sheet is exploratory raster artwork, not a production icon library. Monochrome and tile examples illustrate direction rather than validated small-size exports. A selected mark will need a consistent pixel grid and specifically simplified 16/24/32px variants before integration.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a sophisticated DESIGN EXPLORATION sheet of THREE distinct pixel-art logo concepts for Syphon, a Windows download manager being redesigned in deep black, charcoal, and violet with tactile round controls and crisp pixel-outline icons. Landscape 1536x1024, beautifully restrained dark charcoal presentation background, three equal columns, no UI screenshots or device mockups. Attached image is the CURRENT LOGO REFERENCE: a purple orbital S-shaped ribbon wrapping a dark spherical globe. Reference must be visually understood and recognizable in concept A. This is identity exploration, NOT production icon export. STRICT genuine intentional PIXEL ART: square grid-aligned clusters, stair-step silhouettes, limited 4-color palette, no smooth vector curves, no anti-aliased curvy shapes masquerading as pixels, no voxels/isometric Minecraft blocks, no 3D sphere rendering, no soft fuzzy edge glow, no rainbow purple-blue. Use bright lilac #d694ff, Syphon violet #a34dff, deep purple #7437a3, white monochrome examples. Each column shows a LARGE standalone emblem about 240px wide, under it its same mark WHITE monochrome at 100px, and at bottom same mark in a dark rounded-square app tile 120px wide. All emblems must be legible and distinctive, professional app brand not a pixel game item. Keep reference origin with deliberate redesign and strong negative space. Typography for labels clean modern sans, not pixel font. Plenty of breathing room.
Concept A: '01 — ORBITAL S'. Pixel reinterpretation of current logo. Preserve distinctive S ribbon sweeping diagonally across a round BLACK globe, top and bottom curved arcs rendered as restrained chunky stair steps. Black hollow sphere/circle indicated ONLY by sparse stepped violet contour. ONE S ribbon remains most prominent, widths roughly 3-4 pixels on a coarse 32x32 grid. Simplify current glossy gradients to three discrete violet tones. No tiny inner orbit lines. Make S visually clear and large; keep dark negative space. This is recommended lineage, must feel closest to source.
Concept B: '02 — PIXEL SIPHON'. Fresh download-manager mark: a compact bold pixelated DOWNWARD ARROW feeding into a short open receiver/tray, with a distinctive S-like zigzag cutout in the central shaft. Strong simple 24x24-grid silhouette, unique app emblem, not a generic standalone arrow. Purple and lilac. No circle, no globe, no orb.
Concept C: '03 — CIRCUIT S'. Fresh abstract bold pixel S monogram made from TWO interlocking stepped ribbons with one-pixel negative-space channel. Diagonally moving like linked data, asymmetrically offset corners, strong iconic silhouette. Not a lightning bolt, not dollar sign, not dollar symbol, not chain-link generic. 24x24-grid form. Violet/lilac limited highlights. No circle, no tray.
Top left small title 'SYPHON / PIXEL IDENTITIES'. Under each title a single small descriptor: A 'A familiar orbit, rebuilt in pixels.' B 'A small symbol for bringing things home.' C 'A sharper mark for a new chapter.' No other text, no watermarks, no mock fake brand names. Entire sheet should show three meaningfully different directions while A clearly inherits the reference S wrapped around black globe. Raster precision and consistent intentional pixel sizes across the sheet.
