02 / COMING IN BETA 20

A bit more Syphon.

From the first pasted link to the last byte, keep your downloads within reach.

01From link to your library+

Paste one link or add a batch. Choose where your files go, start them now, or leave them queued for later. Your next download starts on your terms.

02Small windows. Clear signals.+

Keep one download or the whole queue nearby in a floating window. Expand for the details, collapse for the essentials, or follow the activity right from your taskbar.

03A little breathing room+

Set a speed limit when the rest of your connection needs some room. Find finished files in your history, check their details, and open their folder when you need them.
