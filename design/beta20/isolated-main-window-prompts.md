Square 1024x1024 image. App window near-square proportions approximately 900x800 with a small margin all around, NEVER a wide panoramic window. Readable comfortable 16px equivalent body labels and 40px controls. Reference image gives MATERIAL AND COLOR ONLY, redesign main window layout substantially. Neo-noir deep black #050507 surfaces, luminous off-white text, purple #8350C4 accents, lavender gradient highlights, subtle directional hairline reflections. Restrained adaptive violet bloom localized behind the SELECTED active transfer, dim paused row, mostly pure black; avoid glowing border around everything. Windows thin integrated titlebar conventional minimize/maximize/close. Download manager workflows Downloads / History / Settings, New download, Pause all, speed limit. Use consistent understated filetype icons, only video has coastal thumbnail. Do not invent playback. Clear 2 active, 1 paused. Limit metadata to what helps; no excessive columns. Preserve compact readable scale, no tiny dense dashboard, no text wall, no marketing tagline. Small concept name outside window at top. Make the structural layout distinct.
