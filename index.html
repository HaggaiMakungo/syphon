02 / MADE FOR YOUR DOWNLOADS

Keep things moving.

Less digging through folders. Fewer windows to babysit. More time for what you downloaded in the first place.

01Everything in its place+

One link or a whole batch. Send images to Images, archives to Compressed, and keep videos, music, documents and programs in their own folders. Find the finished file from your history.

02Still there. Still downloading.+

Keep one download or the whole queue nearby in a floating window. Expand for the details, collapse for the essentials, or follow the activity right from your taskbar.

03Leave room for the rest+

A download shouldn’t take the whole connection hostage. Set a speed limit, pause what can wait, and resume supported transfers when you’re ready.
